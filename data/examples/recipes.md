# Weekend Recipes

A small collection of easy dishes for two people. Every recipe lists the
ingredients first and the steps after that.

## Pasta with Tomato Sauce

Pasta is the quickest dinner in this collection and needs only one pot
and one pan.

### Ingredients

- 200 g spaghetti
- 1 can of crushed tomatoes
- 2 cloves of garlic and a pinch of salt

### Steps

1. Boil salted water and cook the spaghetti for 9 minutes.
2. Fry the garlic in olive oil, add the tomatoes and simmer for 10 minutes.
3. Mix the pasta into the sauce and serve hot.

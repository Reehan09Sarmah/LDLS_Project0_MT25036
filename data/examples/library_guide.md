# Riverside College Library Guide

This guide explains when the library is open, what you may borrow and how
to use the printers. Riverside College is a made-up example.

## Opening Hours

The library is open every day of the year except three national holidays.

### Weekdays

From Monday to Friday the reading hall opens at 8 am and closes at 10 pm.
The book counter closes earlier, at 6 pm.

### Weekends

On Saturday and Sunday the library opens at 10 am and closes at 6 pm.
During exam weeks it stays open until midnight on both days.

## Borrowing Rules

### Students

Students may borrow up to four books at a time for fourteen days.
A late fine of five rupees per book per day is charged.

### Faculty

Faculty members may borrow up to ten books for one full semester.

## Printing

### Colour

Ten rupees per page.

### Black and white

Two rupees per page.
